BUILD PROVENANCE
------------------------------------------------------------
All 30 ratings were collected on a single build, BEFORE the artist
repeat cap (ARTIST_AFFINITY_REPEAT_LIMIT) was introduced. The cap was
added in response to these results and has NOT been tested with
participants. The offline metrics in evaluation_full.txt are from the
post-cap build, so the two files describe different versions: the user
study evaluates the system as it behaved during the sessions below.

DESIGN
------------------------------------------------------------
Within-subjects, blind A/B. Method assigned by block randomisation so
each participant receives an equal number of each condition. In study
mode the app sends no explicit preferences and holds diversity constant
at 0.3, so the only difference between conditions is history-based
adaptation. Explanation and score breakdown are hidden. Participants
rated 'how well did this track fit your session?' on 1-5.
Convenience sample, 3 participants, 2 sessions each.

RESULT
------------------------------------------------------------
Session-aware : mean 2.36  sd 1.60  n=14
Baseline      : mean 4.00  sd 1.55  n=16
Difference    : -1.64 points on a 1-5 scale
Mann-Whitney U = 52.5, p = 0.0100  -> significant at alpha = 0.05
Rank-biserial effect size r = +0.531 (large)

H1 (listeners rate session-aware higher) is REJECTED. The effect is
significant in the OPPOSITE direction: the baseline was preferred.

MECHANISM: ARTIST OVER-REPETITION
------------------------------------------------------------
session_aware  5 distinct artists across 14 tracks
baseline       9 distinct artists across 16 tracks
session_aware  first appearance 2.78 (n=9) | artist repeated within the same session 1.60 (n=5)
baseline       first appearance 4.09 (n=11) | artist repeated within the same session 3.80 (n=5)

The offline session-coherence metric scored this same repetition as a
success (artist affinity 53.3% vs 0% for the baseline). The metric was
measuring a real behaviour but treating more of it as always better -
the accuracy-proxy gap described by McNee, Riedl and Konstan (2006).

DATA QUALITY
------------------------------------------------------------
Rating latency: median 20.8s, 0/29 under 3s, 28/29 at 10s or more
Assignment balance per participant: 5/5, 5/5, 4/6 (a warm-up or skipped
recommendation consumes an assignment without producing a rating).

LIMITATIONS
------------------------------------------------------------
- 3 participants, 30 ratings, convenience sample: indicative, not
  generalisable.
- The catalogue is Last.fm chart-seeded, so most tracks were familiar to
  participants; familiarity may influence ratings independently of fit.
- Ratings are relative to the session, not absolute musical quality.
- No post-session questionnaire was administered, so no claim is made
  about explanation clarity, trust, or usability.
- The artist repeat cap introduced in response to these findings has not
  been re-tested with participants.
